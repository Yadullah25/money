Settings

General

IQD
Available balance = starting balance + income − expenses ± transfers − money set aside for savings.

Categories

Built-in categories can't be changed. Deleting a custom category that already has transactions hides it from new entries but keeps your history intact.

Backup & restore

Your data is stored only on this phone, inside the app. Nothing is sent to any server. If you delete the app, clear Safari website data, or lose the phone, the data is gone, so download a backup regularly and keep it in the Files app or iCloud.

⬇ Download database backup ⬇ All transactions (CSV)
Replaces everything currently in the app. A safety copy of the current data is saved first on this phone.

Sample data

Load made-up shifts, fuel fill-ups, budgets and goals to explore the app. Sample entries are marked demo. “Remove sample data” deletes only those entries. Anything you added yourself, or edited after loading, is kept.

Password

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never as plain text. The password only matters if other devices can reach the app (e.g. your phone on Wi-Fi). Anyone with access to the files on this computer can still read the database. It is not encrypted.

Open on your phone

Start the app with run_on_wifi.bat (Windows), then on your phone (same Wi-Fi) open http://YOUR-PC-IP:5000. Find the IP with ipconfig → “IPv4 Address”. Set a password first.

Tip: in your phone browser choose “Add to Home screen” to open it like an app.
